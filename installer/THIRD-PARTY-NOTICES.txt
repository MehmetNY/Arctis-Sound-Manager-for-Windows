Arctis Sound Manager for Windows - third-party components shipped with the installer
=====================================================================================

Arctis Sound Manager (ASM) is free software, GPL-3.0-or-later. Its Windows port bundles the following.

Nothing virtual is installed
  ASM adds no audio driver and no virtual sound device to Windows, and it installs no third-party audio software.
  The processed microphone reaches other programs through a small audio effect of ASM's own (asm_micfx.dll, GPL-3.0)
  that Windows' audio engine runs on the headset's own microphone; it can be switched off in the Devices window and is
  removed by the uninstaller, which also restores the Windows setting it needs ("DisableProtectedAudioDG").
